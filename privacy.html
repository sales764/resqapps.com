Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to RESQ+ Emergency Response System ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy with a PRIVACY-FIRST approach. This Privacy Policy explains our minimal data collection practices and how we respect your personal information when you use our mobile application.

🔒 Core Privacy Principle: Emergency profile information is encrypted on your device and securely backed up to your private account (Google Firebase), protected so that only you can access it — your profile survives a lost, broken, or replaced phone. Connected features may process operational data for sign-in, alerts, notifications, media attachments, and AI responses.

⚠️ Important: RESQ+ is an emergency response support application and should not replace professional emergency services. Always call local emergency numbers (Police, Ambulance, Fire) in life-threatening situations.

2. Your Emergency Profile — Encrypted and Backed Up

RESQ+ stores your core emergency profile encrypted on your device (AES-256), and keeps a secure copy in your private account so it is never lost. Only your authenticated account can read it. This data can include:

  • Emergency Profile Details: Blood type, allergies, medications, insurance details, emergency contacts, and other profile fields you enter.
  • App Preferences: Language, SOS settings, and other on-device configuration preferences.
  • Local Media and Recordings: Emergency recordings and local files saved on your device unless you choose to share or upload them through a supported feature.
  • On-Device Access: You can review or remove local profile data directly from the app and by uninstalling the app.

3. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep each type of data only as long as it serves the purpose it was collected for. Automated jobs enforce the limits below every day; none of it depends on a manual clean-up.

  • Emergency profile and account data: Kept for as long as your account exists, because its whole purpose is to be available at any moment during an emergency. It is deleted when you delete your profile in the app, or when you delete your account.
  • Photos, voice notes and videos attached to community or team alerts: Automatically deleted 7 days after upload.
  • Resolved community alerts: Automatically deleted 7 days after they are resolved.
  • SOS recordings (video fragments and the consolidated incident file): Automatically deleted 30 days after the SOS session. This longer window exists so the recording stays available to you, to the emergency contacts you authorised, and to the authorities if an incident has to be documented.
  • Encrypted database backups: Our database keeps a rolling 7-day recovery window, used only to restore data after an incident or an accidental deletion. Older backup states are discarded automatically.
  • Subscription records: Kept while your subscription is active, and afterwards only where accounting or tax law requires it.

You can ask us to delete your data at any time using the contact details in section 11. We do not keep personal data for undefined periods, and we do not sell or share it for advertising.

4. Data Processed for Connected Features

When you use connected RESQ+ features, related operational data may be processed to make those features work. This can include:

  • Phone Authentication Data: Your phone number and authentication events when you sign in with phone verification.
  • Location Data While Active: Location data when you actively use SOS, Team Connect, Global Emergency Directory, weather, or nearby-service features.
  • Alert and Team Data: Team membership, alert responses, community alerts, notification tokens, and related cloud records needed for coordination features.
  • Optional Media and Attachments: Voice, video, image, or other files you choose to upload or attach through supported features.
  • AI Requests: Text, image, and related context sent to generate First Aid Assistant guidance.
  • Analytics and Diagnostics: App analytics, performance signals, and technical diagnostics used to operate and improve the service.

5. App Permissions

RESQ+ requests the following permissions to provide emergency features:

  • LOCATION: For SOS location sharing, Team GPS tracking, Global Emergency Directory, and Compass tools.
  • SEND_SMS: To send emergency SMS alerts when you enable the feature and grant SMS permission.
  • STORAGE: To save local profile data, recordings, and other files created by the app.
  • INTERNET: For First Aid Assistant, sign-in, team sync, cloud alerts, and weather data.
  • NOTIFICATIONS: For team, community, and emergency-related notifications.

You can revoke permissions anytime in Android settings. Some features may not work without necessary permissions.

7. Third-Party Services

RESQ+ uses the following third-party services to support connected features:

  • Google Analytics: App usage and engagement analytics
  • Firebase Services: Sign-in, notifications, alert and team data, optional media uploads, and related cloud features
  • Google Gemini API: First Aid Assistant responses and optional image analysis
  • Weather Data Providers: Forecast condition data used by weather-related tools

These services process the data needed to operate their related features under their own service terms and policies.

8. Children's Privacy

RESQ+ is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have data from a child, contact us and we will delete it immediately.

9. Your Rights

You remain in control of the information stored on your device and the permissions granted to the app:

  • Access: All your data is already on your device (Settings > Emergency Profile)
  • Delete: Delete your profile anytime from app settings
  • Export: Export supported profile data from the app when export tools are available
  • Uninstall: Deleting the app removes all local data

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted in the app with the revision date. Continued use means you accept the new policy.

Google Play — Accessibility Services Disclosure

12. Use of the Android Accessibility API

RESQ+ exposes an optional feature that uses Android's Accessibility API to detect a simultaneous long-press of the Volume + and Volume − hardware buttons as a panic gesture. This document discloses, in full transparency, the precise behaviour and data flow of that feature so that users can give informed consent before enabling it.

12.1 Opt-in only

The Accessibility-based trigger is disabled by default. It is only activated when the user (a) flips a dedicated toggle inside RESQ+ Settings, (b) reads and accepts an in-app "Prominent Disclosure" dialog, and (c) manually enables the RESQ+ Accessibility service in Android System Settings → Accessibility. All three steps are required. The toggle can be reversed at any time from the same Settings screen, which clears the consent flag instantly.

12.2 Purpose — who this feature is for

The feature exists to help users who cannot reach or operate the in-app SOS button reliably, including:

  • users with motor or cognitive impairment who can press hardware keys but cannot navigate touch-screen UIs under stress;
  • users physically restrained by an aggressor and unable to look at the screen;
  • users in low-light or zero-visibility environments;
  • users whose phone is in a pocket, bag or holster.

The Accessibility API is the only Android mechanism that allows hardware-key detection while the screen is locked. No alternative API offers this capability for third-party apps.

12.3 What data the service accesses

When enabled, the RESQ+ Accessibility service reads only:

  • The KEY_DOWN and KEY_UP events of KEYCODE_VOLUME_UP and KEYCODE_VOLUME_DOWN only;
  • The timestamps of those two key transitions, used solely to measure the 2-second and 5-second hold thresholds.

12.4 What the service NEVER accesses

RESQ+ does not, under any circumstance, access:

  • screen content, window content, view hierarchies or UI dumps;
  • text fields, passwords, form inputs, clipboard or selection events;
  • notifications from other apps or system notifications;
  • the list of installed apps, app usage, or foreground app data;
  • browsing history, search queries or URLs;
  • any AccessibilityEvent other than the two hardware-volume key events;
  • biometric data, fingerprints, face data or authentication state.

The Accessibility service is configured with the narrowest possible event mask (flagRequestFilterKeyEvents only — no typeWindowContentChanged, no typeViewTextChanged, no typeNotificationStateChanged).

12.5 On-device processing

Volume-key detection runs 100% on-device. No keypress, timestamp or accessibility event is ever uploaded to RESQ+ servers, Firebase, analytics or any third party. The 10-second pre-roll buffer used to capture the trigger gesture lives exclusively in RAM and is discarded if no SOS is launched.

Only when an SOS is explicitly triggered by the user (via the 2-second hold, the in-app button, the Quick Settings Tile, or auto-escalation by the on-device fall detector) does RESQ+ start uploading the encrypted event log and 4-second video fragments to Firebase Cloud Storage, restricted to the user's authorised emergency contacts and certified RESQ+ rescue operators.

12.6 No misuse — Play Store policy alignment

RESQ+ confirms that its Accessibility service is used exclusively to assist users with the panic gesture described above. It is never used for: advertising, analytics, tracking, automation of other apps, scraping content, capturing credentials, password autofill, in-app purchase manipulation, or any usage that would violate the Google Play Accessibility API policy.

12.7 How to disable

You may revoke consent at any time using either of the following methods (a single one is sufficient):

  • open RESQ+ → Settings → toggle Volume Buttons SOS Trigger to OFF;
  • open Android System Settings → Accessibility → RESQ+ Volume SOS Trigger → OFF;
  • uninstall RESQ+.
