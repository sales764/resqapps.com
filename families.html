When you press SOS

📲

Everyone is alerted at once

Your emergency contacts get a text message and an email with your position (on iPhone, you press Send in Messages). Your family circle gets a notification in the app.

🌐

They follow you live

The alert opens a live page with your position and the video your SOS records. It works in any browser, with nothing to install.

📞

Help close to you

The Thai emergency numbers are ready to call, with embassy contacts. Everyone uses RESQ+ in English, French or Thai.

To look after each other every day

The SOS stays free for everyone. Premium Family adds, for up to 6 people:

  • ★ Circle map: see where everyone is
  • ★ Places with arrival and departure alerts
  • ★ Meeting points with everyone's arrival time
  • ★ 30-day trip history
  • ★ Medical profiles for the whole family
  • ★ First Aid Assistant: by voice, text or photo

The Family plan covers you and 5 people in your circle (the Assistant stays with the subscriber). Cancel anytime in Google Play or the App Store.

Someone back home can pay for it

  1. You install RESQ+ for free and create your family circle.
  2. You invite your children, your partner or your parents, wherever they live. They install RESQ+ and join your circle.
  3. One of them subscribes to Premium Family on their own phone, in Google Play or the App Store. Premium then covers up to 6 people in the circle, including the subscriber.

Any member of the circle can be the one who pays. The SOS stays free for everyone.
