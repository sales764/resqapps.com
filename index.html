What RESQ+ does

Help reaches you faster

Everything that protects you in an emergency is free. Premium adds tools to look after your family every day.

🚨

SOS, your way

Press the SOS button, press the power button four times (even from your pocket, on supported phones), use your Wear OS watch or a Quick Settings tile.

  • ✓ 10 seconds to cancel a false alarm
  • ✓ Discreet silent SOS or loud alarm
  • ✓ Text message from your own SIM, no data needed
  • ✓ Free, always
📍

A live page, no app needed

Anyone with your link follows your position and the video as it records. No app, no account.

  • ✓ Live GPS position and map
  • ✓ Video uploaded in short clips
  • ✓ Full video saved on your phone
  • ✓ One tap to call local emergency services
👨‍👩‍👧

Family circles

Invite your family by QR code or link. Your SOS reaches them as a priority alert, and they reply "On my way!" from the notification.

  • ✓ Up to 50 people per circle
  • ✓ Voice, photo and video chat
  • ✓ Premium: circle map, places with arrival alerts
  • ✓ Premium: meeting points and 30-day trip history
⏱️

Arrival check-in

Going somewhere? Set your arrival time. If you don't confirm you arrived safely, RESQ+ starts a countdown, then sends a silent SOS.

  • ✓ Late nights, dates, solo trips
  • ✓ Works even if you cannot reach your phone
  • ✓ Free
🩹

First aid, even offline

18 first-aid cards (choking, burns, stroke, heart attack, anaphylaxis, seizure, drowning, snake bite, heatstroke…) and a CPR metronome.

  • ✓ Works without internet
  • ✓ My First Aid Kit: expiry dates and reminders
  • ✓ Premium: First Aid Assistant, by voice or text, in EN, FR or TH
  • ✓ Guidance only, not a medical device
🪪

Medical QR for rescuers

Blood type, allergies, medications and contacts in one profile. Your QR code opens with any phone camera, even offline.

  • ✓ You choose what is shown
  • ✓ Encrypted on your phone, backed up to your account
  • ✓ One profile free, more with Premium
🌍

Emergency numbers that follow you

RESQ+ detects the country you are in and shows the right numbers: ambulance, police, fire, mental-health and child-protection lines.

  • ✓ 50+ countries and 1,150 regions
  • ✓ Nearest hospitals and police, with the route
  • ✓ Embassy and consulate lists for travellers
🔒

Private by design

No ads and no advertising ID. Your location is shared only when you decide: during an SOS or with your circle.

  • ✓ The Assistant sees your medical record only with your consent
  • ✓ Delete your account from the app anytime

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about RESQ+

💰

How much does RESQ+ cost?

RESQ+ is free to download, with no ads. Every SOS trigger, the live page, first-aid cards, one medical profile and family circles are free. Premium is optional and adds the circle map with places and 30-day trip history, meeting points, extra medical profiles and the First Aid Assistant. The Family plan extends Premium to 5 people in your circle; the First Aid Assistant stays with the subscriber. Manage or cancel anytime in Google Play.
🌐

Does it work offline?

Yes for the essentials: first-aid cards, the CPR metronome, your medical QR code and the emergency numbers work without internet. The SOS text message needs only mobile signal, not data. Live video, family circles, the First Aid Assistant and community alerts need a connection.
🔒

Is my medical data safe?

Your emergency profile is encrypted on your device and securely backed up to your private account — only you can access it, and it survives a lost or broken phone. When you use connected features such as sign-in, alerts, notifications, team sync, or AI guidance, related operational data may be processed by connected cloud services.
🤖

Is the First Aid Assistant a doctor?

No. It gives calm, step-by-step first aid guidance and tells you when to call emergency services. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose. In a life-threatening emergency, call your local emergency number first (1669 in Thailand, 112 or 911 elsewhere).
📱

Which devices are supported?

Android phones and tablets running Android 7.0 or later. The Wear OS app (Wear OS 3 and later, with the phone app) adds an SOS tile to your watch.
🌍

What languages are supported?

The app currently supports English, French, and Thai. First Aid Assistant guidance is fully available in these supported languages.
👥

How many people can join a family circle?

Up to 50 people per circle, invited by QR code or link. With the Family plan, Premium covers you and 5 members of your circle, except the First Aid Assistant, which stays personal.
🔋

Does it drain battery?

RESQ+ is designed to be light on battery, and location sharing stays under your control. On some phones you may need to allow RESQ+ to run in the background so your SOS always works.
🚨

What happens when I press SOS?

Your emergency contacts get a text message with your position and a link to your live page, the family circles you choose get a priority alert and, if you allow it, RESQ+ users nearby are asked to help. Video is uploaded in short clips and the full video stays on your phone. RESQ+ alerts your people; it does not call emergency services for you.
📊

What data do you collect?

RESQ+ processes the data needed to deliver connected features such as sign-in, alerts, notifications, and AI responses. Emergency profile details stay on your device unless you choose to share information through supported features.
